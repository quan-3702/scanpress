How to use ScanPress

1. Add pages

Click ＋ New scan and choose scans or photos of documents (JPG, PNG, WEBP…) – or a scanned PDF (Pro). You can also drop files on the window or take pictures with 📷 Camera. Each picture becomes a page; drag the small pages to change the order.

2. The text is read automatically

Choose the text language (up to two, e.g. English and Vietnamese). The first time, the reading data for a language is downloaded once (a few MB). Crooked pages are straightened automatically. If a page is upside down, turn it with ⟲ ⟳; use ✂ Crop to cut away the table or background; Look makes the page look like a clean scan, grey or black & white.

The text appears on the right. You can correct it there – Word and text files use your corrections.

3. Save

⬇ Save makes a searchable PDF (the picture of the page with invisible text, so you can search, select and copy), a Word file (Pro) or a text file. 📋 Copy all text copies everything.

Free and Pro

Free: documents of up to 3 pages, pictures and camera, all languages, searchable PDF (with a small “Made with ScanPress” line), text. Pro (one-time purchase): documents of any length, scanned PDFs, Word, search inside all documents, many files at once, clean PDFs. Try Pro free for 7 days from ☰.
