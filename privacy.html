ScanPress – Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. ScanPress is made by NTQ37.

Your documents stay on your computer

The text reader (OCR) runs on your own computer. Your scans, photos, PDFs, the recognised text and your corrections are stored only in this app on your device. They are never uploaded to NTQ37 or anyone else.

What is downloaded

For each language you choose, the open-source reading data (a few MB) is downloaded once from jsDelivr. It contains no information about you or your documents. After that ScanPress works offline.

Camera

The camera is used only while the camera window is open, after you allow it. Pictures are saved in the app on your computer only.

Purchases

ScanPress Pro is bought through the Microsoft Store. NTQ37 does not receive your payment details; the app only asks the Store whether Pro is owned.

No accounts, no tracking

There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.
